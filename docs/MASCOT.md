# Pebble, the mascot

Pebble is an original character made for this app: a small round seed-bird.

- **Shape:** one big circle body, a cream oval belly, two small oval wings, two short feet.
- **Face:** two dark oval eyes with a white glint, a small orange triangle beak, soft pink cheeks. No eyebrows,
  no teeth, no frown: Pebble never looks cross.
- **Signature:** a single green leaf sprouting from the top of the head, which sways when Pebble moves.
- **Palette:** body `#5FB49C` (sea green), wings `#4E9C85`, belly `#FFF4DC`, beak and feet `#F4A259`,
  leaf `#8CC84B`, cheeks `#F6B8B0`, eyes `#2E3A3F`.
- **Moods:** Idle (gentle bob and blink), Celebrate (happy closed eyes, wings up, little hop),
  Encourage (open beak, leaf lean, slight tilt), Sleep (closed eyes, slow breathing; the rest screen).
- **Voice:** warm and slow. Pebble only praises, invites and names things. It never says "no" or "wrong".

Pebble is drawn in code (`ui/components/Pebble.kt`) on a 100 x 100 grid and as the launcher icon
(`res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml`). It is not based on any existing character.
