# Tester guide

Thank you for helping test **Toddler English**, a free app where children aged 1 to 5 hear and play with their
first English words. This guide takes about 15 minutes the first time, then a few minutes on any day you play.

## Joining
1. Open the opt-in link you were sent, on your Android phone or tablet, signed in with the Google account you
   gave us.
2. Tap **Become a tester**, then **Download it on Google Play** and install.
3. **Please stay opted in for at least 14 days.** Google requires 12 or more testers for 14 days in a row before
   the app can be published. You do not need to play every day, just keep the app installed and stay opted in.

## Privacy while testing
- The app collects nothing and has no internet access; we cannot see how you or your child use it.
- When you send feedback, **do not include your child's name, photos or videos of your child**. Screenshots of
  the app are welcome.

## Tip: keep little fingers in the app
Back does not leave the app, but the Home button does. To lock the app on screen, turn on **Settings > Security
> App pinning** (or "Screen pinning"), open Toddler English, open Recents, tap the app icon and choose **Pin**.

## What to try
Tick what works and note anything odd.

**First run**
- [ ] Pebble says hello when the app opens.
- [ ] Tapping Pebble makes Pebble hop and talk.

**Scenes** (Farm, Food, Home, Bedtime, Outdoors)
- [ ] Tapping a thing says its name, makes it wiggle and shows the word.
- [ ] Tapping empty space makes a little sparkle.
- [ ] The house button and the phone's Back button go back to the home screen.

**Games** (home screen > Games)
- [ ] Bubbles: popping a bubble says the word.
- [ ] Find it: Pebble asks for a picture; the right one gets a cheer; another one is simply named.
- [ ] Feed Pebble: the food flies to Pebble and Pebble says "Yum yum!".
- [ ] Songs: the picture and the words follow the song.

**Stickers**
- [ ] After tapping five different things in one scene, a sticker flies in.
- [ ] The sticker appears in the sticker book.

**Grown-up area** (padlock on the home screen)
- [ ] A quick tap does not open it. Holding the circle for 3 seconds shows a sum written in words.
- [ ] The right answer opens the parent corner. A wrong one starts again.
- [ ] Try each word level and check the games get a little harder.
- [ ] Set the play time to 5 minutes, play, and check that Pebble goes to sleep and play stops. Only the
  padlock leads back.
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode and check everything still works.

## Questions for you (any time)
1. Did your child understand what to do without help? Where did they get stuck?
2. Were any words hard to hear or wrongly pronounced?
3. Did anything feel too fast, too busy or too quiet?
4. Did the app ever close, freeze or show a blank screen? What were you doing?
5. Your phone model and Android version (Settings > About phone).

## Sending feedback
Use the feedback link in the Play Store testing page, or email the developer contact address you were given.
Thank you!
