# Toddler English: privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Toddler English is a free app that helps children aged 1 to 5 learn English words. It is made by
**[developer name]** ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: **the app
collects nothing, and nothing leaves your device.**

## What the app collects
Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information, from children or
from adults. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

## Network access
The app does not use the internet. It does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send or
receive data online. All words, pictures, sounds and songs are inside the app.

## Information kept only on your device
To work, the app keeps a few things in its own private storage on your phone or tablet:
- settings chosen in the parent corner (sound on or off, word level, play-time limit);
- simple play counts: which words were heard and how often, game rounds played, songs finished, stickers earned,
  and minutes played.

This information never leaves the device and we cannot see it. It is not included in Google cloud backups.
A grown-up can erase it at any time with **Parent corner > Reset progress**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Microphone, camera, location and contacts
The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or any other sensitive permission.

## Children
The app is designed for young children and follows Google Play's Families policy. Because it collects no
personal information, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete on any server. Parts of the app meant
for grown-ups (settings, progress, closing the app) are behind a parental gate.

## Third parties
The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social-media code. It uses only Google's Android
libraries (AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Media3) to draw the screens and play bundled sounds; none of these send
data from the app.
